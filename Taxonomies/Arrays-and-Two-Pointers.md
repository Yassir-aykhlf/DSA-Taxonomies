> **Related Patterns**:
> - Hashing & Grouping (seen-value maps and frequency counts support lookup and complement matching)
> - Sorting (ordered values guide k-sum pointer moves and sorted-sequence merges)
> - Sliding Window (two boundary pointers maintain a contiguous span)
> - Linked List (two-pointer merging also combines sorted node chains)
