> **Related Patterns**:
> - Bit Manipulation (binary decomposition accelerates exponentiation and division)
> - Hashing & Grouping (maps record repeated remainders, normalized slopes, and point counts)
> - Sorting (ordering points or angles supports convex hulls and geometric sweeps)
> - Binary Search (monotonic counting predicates locate the n-th value meeting a divisibility rule)
